My study method

A repeatable approach to technical exams.

  1. Read the official skills outline first.
  2. Map every objective to a learning resource.
  3. Practise in a sandbox or guided lab where possible.
  4. Write short notes in your own words.
  5. Use practice questions to identify gaps, not just memorise.
  6. Revisit weak areas and verify exam-day requirements.
