Certification study page

DP-600 · Microsoft Fabric Analytics Engineer Associate

Current certification. A practical learning page for understanding the credential and organising your preparation.

Intermediate100 minutesRegional price shown by Microsoft at booking

What this exam focuses on

Use Microsoft's official skills outline as the source of truth.

  • Maintain an analytics solution, prepare data, and implement and manage semantic models
  • The role includes analytical assets such as semantic models, warehouses and lakehouses; SQL, KQL and DAX are relevant

Suggested study approach

A learning sequence, not a substitute for the official exam objectives.

  1. Understand lakehouses, warehouses and Fabric analytical assets.
  2. Practise preparing and enriching data for analytics.
  3. Build and optimise semantic models; understand Direct Lake in context.
  4. Study SQL, DAX and KQL for analytics tasks.
  5. Review security, governance, monitoring and lifecycle management.
