PL-900 study plan: a structured way to prepare

A reusable study framework for Power Platform Fundamentals.

FabricSafar · Beginner-friendly · Starter guide

PL-900 is Microsoft Power Platform Fundamentals. This is a study framework, not a substitute for the current official skills outline. Exam objectives can change, so begin with the current Microsoft exam page.

Build your topic checklist

  • Describe the business value of Power Platform.
  • Identify core Power Apps capabilities and app types.
  • Understand Power Automate and common automation scenarios.
  • Recognise Power Pages use cases.
  • Understand Copilot Studio fundamentals.
  • Know the role of Dataverse and connectors.

A two-week revision rhythm

  1. Days 1–3: platform overview, business value and components.
  2. Days 4–6: Power Apps, app types and Dataverse.
  3. Days 7–9: Power Automate, triggers, actions and connectors.
  4. Days 10–11: Power Pages and Copilot Studio.
  5. Days 12–13: scenario questions and weak areas.
  6. Day 14: final review and exam logistics.

Exam-day approach

Read each scenario carefully, identify the business requirement and eliminate options that solve a different problem. Understand when each product is appropriate rather than memorising definitions alone.

PL-900 certification page · Official study guide

Learning tip: turn each concept into a small example, then explain it in your own words. That is where the understanding sticks.
