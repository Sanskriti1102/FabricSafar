Getting to know Microsoft Power Platform

By Sanskriti Kadam
FabricSafar · Author

A high-level map of the main Power Platform products.

FabricSafar · Beginner-friendly · Starter guide

Microsoft Power Platform is a collection of low-code tools and services that help organisations build apps, automate processes, analyse data and create conversational experiences.

Main products

  • Power Apps: build business applications. Canvas apps offer control over the interface; model-driven apps are built around data and the Dataverse model.
  • Power Automate: automate workflows across apps and services.
  • Power Pages: create external-facing business websites.
  • Copilot Studio: build and manage agents and conversational experiences.
  • Power BI: analyse data and communicate insights.

Where does Dataverse fit?

Microsoft Dataverse is a data platform used by Power Platform solutions to securely store and manage business data. It provides tables, relationships, business rules and security capabilities.

Choose based on the problem

For a business app, explore Power Apps. For a repetitive cross-service process, consider Power Automate. For an external portal, explore Power Pages. For analysis and reporting, consider Power BI.

Power Platform documentation

Learning tip: turn each concept into a small example, then explain it in your own words. That is where the understanding sticks.
