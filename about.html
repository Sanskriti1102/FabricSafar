About FabricSafar

A learning journey through data, automation and the Microsoft technology ecosystem — documented one concept at a time.

Why FabricSafar exists

FabricSafar is an independent technical learning publication created to make Microsoft technology concepts easier to explore. It brings together beginner-friendly explanations, practical walkthroughs, study guidance and notes from hands-on learning.

✎

Clear explanations

Break complex concepts into approachable, structured notes.

⌘

Practical learning

Connect theory with examples, projects and experiments.

✦

Study guidance

Share roadmaps, revision frameworks and certification experiences.

Editorial promise

  • Distinguish personal notes from official product guidance.
  • Link to primary documentation where possible.
  • Show when an article was last reviewed.
  • Update content when products or exam objectives change.
  • Keep the publication independent and educational.
Independent learning resource: FabricSafar is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by Microsoft. Product names and marks belong to their respective owners. Verify current product details and exam objectives using official Microsoft documentation.
